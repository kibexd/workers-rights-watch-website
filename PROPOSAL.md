## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why is ongoing maintenance necessary?
**A:** Websites need regular updates for security, compatibility, and performance. Without maintenance, websites break, get hacked, or become slow. It's like maintaining a vehicle - regular service prevents major problems.

### Q: Can I do maintenance myself?
**A:** Basic content updates can be done by you with training. However, technical updates (security patches, code updates, server management) require developer expertise. Most organizations find outsourcing more cost-effective than hiring technical staff.

### Q: What if I only want hosting without maintenance?
**A:** This is possible but not recommended. A hosted website without maintenance will eventually have security vulnerabilities, broken features, or outdated content. Minimum recommended: quarterly maintenance checks (KES 15,000 per quarter).

### Q: How long until I see SEO results?
**A:** SEO is a marathon, not a sprint. Expect:
- Month 1-3: Foundation building, minimal visible results
- Month 3-6: First ranking improvements, small traffic increase
- Month 6-12: Significant improvements, steady traffic growth
- Year 2+: Strong rankings, consistent traffic, best ROI

### Q: What happens if I stop paying for maintenance?
**A:** Your website continues to work as-is. However:
- No security updates (vulnerability risk)
- No bug fixes
- No content updates
- No technical support
- SEO work stops (rankings may drop)
- You can restart service anytime

### Q: Can another developer take over later?
**A:** Yes! Your website uses standard technology. Any competent web developer can maintain it. I provide full documentation and source code. However, there will be a learning curve for new developers (2-4 weeks typically).

### Q: Do you offer training for my team?
**A:** Yes! Initial handoff includes 1-2 hours of training for basic updates:

Additional training: KES 5,000 per 2-hour session

### Q: What if I need a feature that's not included?

- Complex features: KES 80,000 - 200,000+
Examples: Blog system, member portal, event registration, online courses, etc.

### Q: Can I switch to a cheaper developer for maintenance?
