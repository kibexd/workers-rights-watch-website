## Tips for Sending Messages

### Timing
- **Best time:** 9 AM - 11 AM or 2 PM - 4 PM (business hours)
- **Avoid:** Early morning (before 8 AM), late evening (after 6 PM), weekends
- **Follow-up:** Wait 24-48 hours before sending follow-up

### Tone
- Professional but friendly
- Clear and concise
- No pressure, but firm on terms
- Always end with your name and contact

### What to Avoid
- Don't sound desperate
- Don't be too pushy
- Don't accept verbal promises without written agreement
- Don't release website before securing all cheques

### When to Call Instead
- If no response after 2 SMS messages
- If she has questions about the payment plan
- If there's a payment issue
- To schedule the signing meeting

## Quick Reference: What to Say When She Calls

### If she says "Can I just give 2 cheques?"
**Response:** "I understand, but I need all 3 cheques upfront for security. This is standard practice. The post-dated cheques won't be deposited until the dates specified. It protects both of us."

### If she says "Why can't the website go live fully now?"
**Response:** "The website will go live after your first payment clears. However, full ownership transfers only after complete payment. This is standard in the industry and protects both parties. You'll have full access to show donors and use the site."

### If she says "Can we reduce the total amount?"
**Response:** "The KES 85,000 is already a fair mid-market rate for a 10-page custom website with advanced features. Agencies charge KES 200,000-500,000 for similar work. However, the payment plan makes it easier - KES 25k, 25k, 35k over 5 months."

### If she says "What if I can't pay by May?"
**Response:** "I understand budget constraints. If you need more time, we can discuss an extension before May. However, late payments incur a 5% monthly fee as per the contract. Let's plan ahead to avoid that."

### If she asks "What happens if a cheque bounces?"
**Response:** "If a cheque bounces, there's a KES 2,500 processing fee and you'll need to provide a replacement within 7 days. Website access may be suspended until payment clears. But I'm confident this won't happen - just ensure funds are available on deposit dates."

---

**Remember:** Be professional, firm, but understanding. You're protecting your work while helping her organization.
