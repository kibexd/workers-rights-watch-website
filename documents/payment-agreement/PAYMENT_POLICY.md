# PAYMENT & WEBSITE RELEASE POLICY

**Enock Kibe Ngunyi - Web Development Services**

**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## PURPOSE

This policy outlines the standard payment terms, website release procedures, and ownership transfer guidelines for all web development projects undertaken by Enock Kibe Ngunyi.

---

## POLICY STATEMENT

All web development projects follow a **staged payment and release model** to protect both the developer and the client. Website access and ownership transfer are tied directly to payment milestones.

---

## 1. PAYMENT TERMS

### 1.1 Standard Payment Structure

**Option A: 50/50 Split (Standard)**
- 50% deposit upon project commencement
- 50% upon project completion and approval
- Total project fee as agreed in contract

**Option B: Installment Plan (By Agreement)**
- Multiple payments spread over agreed timeline
- All post-dated cheques/payment commitments provided upfront
- Minimum 30% deposit required to commence work
- Final payment due within 6 months of project start

### 1.2 Accepted Payment Methods

1. **Cheque** (Preferred for installment plans)
   - Current cheques deposited immediately
   - Post-dated cheques held until specified date
   - All cheques must be provided upfront for installment plans

2. **Bank Transfer**
   - Immediate confirmation required
   - Bank charges borne by client

3. **M-Pesa**
   - Suitable for smaller payments
   - Confirmation SMS required

4. **Cash** (For amounts under KES 50,000)
   - Receipt provided immediately
   - Counted and verified in presence of both parties

### 1.3 Payment Security for Installment Plans

For projects with multiple payments:
- **All payment instruments (cheques) must be provided upfront**
- Post-dated cheques held securely until deposit date
- Client notified 7 days before depositing post-dated cheques
- Signed Payment Commitment Letter required
- Clear payment schedule documented in contract

## 2. WEBSITE RELEASE POLICY

### 2.1 Staged Release Model

All projects follow a **three-stage release process** tied to payment milestones:

#### STAGE 1: Test/Preview Release
**Trigger:** First payment received and cleared

**What Client Gets:**
- ✅ Website live on test/temporary domain
- ✅ Full viewing access
- ✅ All features functional
- ✅ Can showcase to stakeholders
- ✅ Limited content update access (with developer assistance)

**What Client DOES NOT Get:**
- ❌ Independent admin access
- ❌ Website on main domain
- ❌ Full ownership rights
- ❌ Source code access

**Developer Retains:**
- Full admin control
- Hosting control
- Domain control
- Ability to suspend access if subsequent payments fail

#### STAGE 2: Main Domain Release
**Trigger:** Second payment received and cleared (50% of total paid)

**What Client Gets:**
- ✅ Website on main domain (e.g., clientdomain.com)
- ✅ SSL certificate activated
- ✅ Limited admin access for content management
- ✅ Ability to update text, images, documents
- ✅ Training on basic updates

**What Client DOES NOT Get:**
- ❌ Full system admin access
- ❌ Complete ownership
- ❌ Access to sensitive settings
- ❌ Source code

**Developer Retains:**
- Root/system admin access
- Hosting control
- Critical system settings control
- Ability to suspend for non-payment

#### STAGE 3: Full Ownership Transfer
**Trigger:** Final payment received and cleared (100% paid)

**What Client Gets:**
- ✅ Full admin access (all levels)
- ✅ Complete ownership of website
- ✅ All source code and documentation
- ✅ Hosting transfer (if desired)
- ✅ Domain transfer (if applicable)
- ✅ All passwords and credentials
- ✅ Training session (2 hours)
- ✅ 30-day free support period begins

**Developer Retains:**
- Right to showcase project in portfolio
- No ongoing control or access (unless maintenance agreement)

## 3. OWNERSHIP TRANSFER POLICY

### 3.1 Intellectual Property Rights

**Before Full Payment:**
- Developer retains all intellectual property rights
- Client has usage license only
- Client cannot resell, redistribute, or claim ownership
- Client cannot hire another developer to modify code

**After Full Payment:**
- Complete intellectual property transfer to client
- Client owns 100% of custom code
- Client can modify, distribute, or resell (not recommended for NGOs)
- Open-source libraries remain under their respective licenses

### 3.2 Portfolio Rights

Developer retains the right to:
- Showcase project in portfolio (screenshots, descriptions)
- Use project as reference for future clients
- Mention client name in portfolio (unless confidentiality agreement)

Client can request:
- Delayed portfolio showcase (e.g., 6 months after launch)
- Anonymous showcase (no client name mentioned)
- Complete confidentiality (additional fee may apply)

## 4. LATE PAYMENT POLICY

### 4.1 Late Payment Fees

**Grace Period:** 7 days after due date (no penalty)

**After Grace Period:**
- **5% monthly late fee** on outstanding amount
- Calculated from original due date
- Compounds monthly if unpaid

**Example:**
- Payment due: KES 25,000 on January 31
- Payment made: March 15 (1.5 months late)
- Late fee: KES 1,875 (5% × 1.5 months)
- Total due: KES 26,875

### 4.2 Consequences of Late Payment

**7 days late:**
- Friendly reminder sent
- No service interruption

**14 days late:**
- Formal notice sent
- Late fee applied
- Limited admin access suspended

**30 days late:**
- Website access suspended (displays "Under Maintenance")
- Developer may terminate contract
- All paid amounts non-refundable
- Legal action may be pursued

### 4.3 Payment Plans for Late Payments

If client cannot pay on time:
- Contact developer BEFORE due date
- Negotiate new payment date
- May require additional security (extra post-dated cheque)
- Late fees may be waived if arranged in advance

---

## 5. DISHONORED CHEQUE POLICY

### 5.1 If a Cheque Bounces

**Immediate Actions:**
- Website access suspended within 24 hours
- Client notified via phone and email
- 7-day grace period to provide replacement payment

**Fees:**
- **KES 2,500 processing fee** (covers bank charges and admin time)
- Original payment amount still due
- Late fees apply if beyond original due date

**Replacement Payment:**
- Must be made within 7 days
- Preferably via bank transfer or cash (not cheque)
- If cheque, must be from different account or certified cheque

### 5.2 Repeated Bounced Cheques

**Second Bounce:**
- KES 5,000 processing fee
- All remaining payments must be cash/bank transfer
- May require immediate full payment
- Developer may terminate contract

**Third Bounce:**
- Contract terminated
- Legal action initiated
- All paid amounts non-refundable
- Website taken offline permanently

---

## 6. WEBSITE SUSPENSION POLICY

### 6.1 Reasons for Suspension

Developer may suspend website access for:
1. Non-payment (14+ days overdue)
2. Dishonored cheque (immediate)
3. Breach of contract terms
4. Illegal or harmful content added by client
5. Excessive resource usage without payment

### 6.2 Suspension Process

**Notice Period:**
- 7 days written notice (except for dishonored cheques)
- Notice sent via email and SMS
- Opportunity to resolve issue

**During Suspension:**
- Website displays "Under Maintenance" message
- No data is deleted
- Client cannot access admin panel
- Email forwarding continues (if set up)

**Restoration:**
- Payment received and cleared
- Processing fee paid (if applicable)
- 24-48 hours to restore full access

## 7. CONTRACT TERMINATION POLICY

### 7.1 Client-Initiated Termination

**Before Project Completion:**
- 30 days written notice required
- All paid amounts non-refundable
- Work completed to date remains client's property
- Partial delivery provided (if 50%+ paid)

**After Project Completion (During Payment Plan):**
- 30 days written notice required
- All outstanding payments become immediately due
- Website access suspended after 30 days if unpaid
- No refunds for paid amounts

### 7.2 Developer-Initiated Termination

**Grounds for Termination:**
- Non-payment for 30+ days
- Repeated dishonored cheques (3+)
- Breach of contract terms
- Illegal use of website
- Abusive behavior toward developer

**Process:**
- 14 days written notice
- Opportunity to cure breach
- If not resolved, contract terminated
- All outstanding payments due immediately
- Website taken offline

## 8. REFUND POLICY

### 8.1 No Refunds After Work Commences

**General Rule:** All payments are non-refundable once work has started

**Exceptions:**
1. Developer fails to deliver as promised
2. Developer abandons project
3. Mutual agreement to cancel (negotiated refund)

### 8.2 Partial Refunds

May be considered if:
- Project cancelled before 25% completion
- Developer unable to continue (illness, etc.)
- Major scope change makes project unfeasible

**Refund Calculation:**
- Total paid minus work completed (hourly rate)
- Minus any expenses incurred (hosting, domains, licenses)
- Minus 20% administrative fee

## 9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 9.1 Escalation Process

**Step 1: Direct Communication (Days 1-7)**
- Discuss issue via phone/email
- Attempt to resolve amicably
- Document all communications

**Step 2: Written Negotiation (Days 8-21)**
- Formal written complaint/response
- Propose solutions
- Negotiate terms

**Step 3: Mediation (Days 22-45)**
- Engage neutral third party
- Mediation through professional body
- Costs shared equally

**Step 4: Arbitration (Days 46+)**
- Binding arbitration as per contract
- Nairobi Centre for International Arbitration
- Decision is final

### 9.2 Legal Action

As last resort:
- Either party may pursue legal action
- Kenyan law applies
- Nairobi jurisdiction

## 10. SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES

### 10.1 NGO/Non-Profit Considerations

For registered NGOs:
- Payment plans more flexible
- Longer payment periods considered
- No interest charges (only late fees)
- Understanding of donor funding cycles

### 10.2 Force Majeure

Payment obligations suspended during:
- Natural disasters
- Pandemics
- War or civil unrest
- Government-imposed restrictions

**Process:**
- Notify other party within 7 days
- Provide evidence of force majeure
- Agree on new payment schedule
- No late fees during force majeure period

### 10.3 Financial Hardship

If client faces genuine financial hardship:
- Contact developer immediately
- Provide evidence (if comfortable)
- Negotiate extended payment plan
- May waive some late fees
- Maintain website access during negotiation

### 11.1 To Ensure Smooth Payment Process

✅ **DO:**

- Discuss payment challenges openly
- Request payment plan adjustments early
- Maintain professional communication
- Keep developer updated on funding status
