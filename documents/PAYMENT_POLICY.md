## 4. POST-DATED CHEQUES POLICY

### 4.1 When Required

Post-dated cheques are MANDATORY for:
- All payment plans with deferred payments
- Any payment scheduled more than 7 days in future
- Clients requesting extended payment terms

### 4.2 Post-Dated Cheque Requirements

**All post-dated cheques must:**
- Be provided BEFORE project launch/delivery
- Be drawn on valid bank accounts
- Have correct amounts and dates
- Be signed by authorized signatory
- Include organization stamp (for organizations)
- Be valid for 6 months from issue date

**Number of Cheques Required:**
- One post-dated cheque for EACH future payment
- Example: 3-payment plan = 2 post-dated cheques (excluding deposit)

### 4.3 Cheque Management

**Deposit Schedule:**
- Cheques deposited on exact date written on cheque
- No early deposits without client consent
- Client notified 7 days before deposit date

**Bounced Cheque Policy:**
- KES 2,000 bounced cheque fee applies
- Immediate suspension of services/website access
- Payment must be made within 7 days
- Legal action may be pursued for repeated bounced cheques

## 6. LATE PAYMENT POLICY

### 6.1 Late Payment Fees

**Grace Period:** 7 days after due date (no fee)

**After Grace Period:**
- **Days 8-14:** 5% late fee on outstanding amount
- **Days 15-30:** Additional 5% (total 10%)
- **After 30 days:** Additional 10% (total 20%)

**Example:**
- Amount due: KES 25,000
- 10 days late: KES 25,000 + 5% (KES 1,250) = KES 26,250
- 20 days late: KES 25,000 + 10% (KES 2,500) = KES 27,500
- 35 days late: KES 25,000 + 20% (KES 5,000) = KES 30,000

### 6.2 Consequences of Non-Payment

**After 14 Days Late:**
- Website/service access suspended
- 48-hour notice provided before suspension
- Client notified via phone, email, and SMS

**After 30 Days Late:**
- Contract may be terminated
- All payments made are non-refundable
- Legal action may be initiated
- Project ownership remains with developer

**After 60 Days Late:**
- Matter referred to debt collection
- Legal proceedings initiated
- Credit reference bureaus notified (for businesses)
