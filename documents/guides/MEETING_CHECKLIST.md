## HANDLING OBJECTIONS

### "Can I just give 2 cheques now and the 3rd later?"

**Response:** "I understand, but I need all 3 cheques upfront for security. This is standard practice for installment plans. The 3rd cheque won't be deposited until May 31, so it just sits with me until then. It protects both of us."

### "What if I don't have enough funds in May?"

**Response:** "That's why we're planning 5 months ahead. If you anticipate any issues, please let me know at least 2 weeks before May 31. We can discuss options then. But the cheque commitment now shows we're both serious about this agreement."

### "Can the website go fully live now?"

**Response:** "The website will go live after your first payment clears - that's in 3-5 days. You'll have full viewing access and can show it to anyone. However, full ownership transfers only after complete payment. This is standard in the industry and protects my work."

### "KES 85,000 is too much"

**Response:** "I understand budget concerns. However, this is already a fair mid-market rate for a 10-page custom website. Agencies charge KES 200,000-500,000 for similar work. The payment plan makes it easier - KES 25k, 25k, 35k over 5 months. This is the agreed amount in our original discussions."

### "I need to discuss with the board first"

**Response:** "I understand. How long do you need? Can we schedule another meeting next week? Please note that I'm holding this payment plan offer for 7 days. After that, I may need to review the terms."
