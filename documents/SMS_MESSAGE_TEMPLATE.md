## QUICK REFERENCE: WHICH MESSAGE TO USE WHEN

| Situation | Use | Method |
|-----------|-----|--------|
| Initial proposal | SMS 1 or WhatsApp | WhatsApp (Better) |
| No response after 24h | SMS 2 | SMS |
| Confirming meeting | SMS 3 | SMS |
| After receiving payment | SMS 4 | SMS |
| Cheque cleared | SMS 5 | SMS + Email |
| Payment reminder | SMS 6, 7, 8 | SMS |
| Payment delayed | SMS 9 | SMS + Call |
| Project complete | SMS 10 | SMS + Email |
| Formal communication | Professional Email | Email |

---

**TIPS:**

✅ Use WhatsApp for initial detailed proposal (supports formatting)  
✅ Use SMS for quick reminders and updates  
✅ Use Email for formal documentation  
✅ Always follow up after 24-48 hours if no response  
✅ Be professional but friendly  
✅ Keep messages concise and clear  
✅ Include call-to-action in every message
