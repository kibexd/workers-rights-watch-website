## 💡 Pro Tips

### During Negotiation:
1. **Start high, have room:** You're at KES 180k, can go to KES 150k if needed
2. **Emphasize value:** 10 pages, features, quality vs cheap alternatives
3. **Show comparisons:** Market rates, agency pricing, competitor websites
4. **Bundle smartly:** Annual maintenance saves her money, guarantees you income
5. **Be flexible on structure:** Payment plan, quarterly billing, etc.
6. **Stay firm on minimums:** Don't go below KES 150k + KES 15k/month

### After Getting Paid:
1. **Deliver remaining work quickly:** Build trust for ongoing relationship
2. **Over-communicate:** Send updates, reports, keep her informed
3. **Suggest improvements:** Show you're invested in her success
4. **Document everything:** Keep records of all work done
5. **Ask for testimonial:** After successful launch
6. **Request referrals:** Her network may need websites too
