## What Is This Website?

Your Workers Rights Watch website is a complete online platform that helps you:

✅ **Reach More People**: Anyone in Kenya or worldwide can learn about your work  
✅ **Share Your Impact**: Show the 50,000+ workers you've helped  
✅ **Receive Donations**: Accept contributions from supporters globally  
✅ **Recruit Talent**: Post jobs and receive volunteer applications  
✅ **Educate Communities**: Share reports, articles, videos, and training materials  
✅ **Build Partnerships**: Showcase your network of 18+ partner organizations  
✅ **Stay Connected**: Display real-time updates from your Twitter/X account

### Common Visitor Journeys:

#### Journey 1: Learning About WRW
```
Visitor lands on HOME page
    ↓
Reads about your impact (50,000+ workers helped)
    ↓
Clicks "Our Mission" button
    ↓
Reads ABOUT US page (vision, mission, values)
    ↓
Explores OUR PROGRAMS to see specific initiatives
    ↓
Visits OUR TEAM to meet the people
    ↓
Goes to CONTACT page to get in touch
```

#### Journey 2: Donor Who Wants to Give
```
Visitor lands on HOME page
    ↓
Impressed by impact statistics
    ↓
Clicks "Donate Now" button (green button in nav or bottom section)
    ↓
Arrives at DONATE page
    ↓
Selects amount and currency
    ↓
Chooses payment method (Card/M-Pesa/PayPal)
    ↓
Completes donation
    ↓
Receives email confirmation
```

#### Journey 3: Job Seeker/Volunteer
```
Visitor clicks CAREERS in navigation
    ↓
Sees list of open positions
    ↓
Clicks "View Details & Apply" on interesting job
    ↓
Reads full job description
    ↓
Fills out application form
    ↓
Uploads CV
    ↓
Submits application
    ↓
Receives confirmation email
    ↓
WRW team receives notification
```

#### Journey 4: Researcher Looking for Information
```
Visitor clicks RESOURCES in navigation
    ↓
Sees tabs: Articles, Reports, Videos, Training Manuals, Photos
    ↓
Clicks REPORTS tab
    ↓
Browses available reports with cover images
    ↓
Clicks "Download" on desired report
    ↓
PDF downloads to their device
    ↓
Can also click "View Online" to read in browser
```

#### Journey 5: Social Media Visitor
```
Someone sees your tweet on Twitter/X

Lands on HOME page
    ↓
Scrolls to see Twitter feed section on homepage
```
